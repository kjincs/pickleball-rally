Our approach

We care about the people behind the paddles. This is a place for useful discoveries, welcoming communities, and stories that make you want to play.

01

Read the edit

Short original summaries, a reason to read, and a link to the publisher. Our illustrations are original artwork, not photos of reported events.

02

Leave your review

Use your GitHub account to score a story from 1–5, select what you want more or less of, and leave a comment. Reviews are public on GitHub.

03

Shape the next search

Our scheduled AI research uses invited reviewers’ feedback alongside the editorial brief. It looks for new stories, removes repeats, and prepares the next edition.

04

Build a useful library

Previous editions remain available. Reader preferences guide selection; original sources and dates support the reporting.

AI assists research and summarization; it can make mistakes. Follow the source links for full context. A positive rating measures interest, not factual verification. Product announcements are identified as brand perspectives. Content is editorial, with no paid placement in this pilot.

The daily rhythm

New editions are scheduled for around 8:17 a.m. Central time (GitHub scheduling can be delayed). Reviews received before a run are included in its feedback snapshot; later reviews inform a future edition. If research fails, the last successful edition stays visible with its actual date.
